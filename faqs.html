Frequently Asked
Questions

What all do you offer?

Psyke! offers Psychology NCERT notes, concept charts, revision-friendly study resources, psychology articles, and a space for young writers to share their work.

What makes you different?

Psyke! is student-led and designed with students in mind—clear resources, approachable explanations, and thoughtful writing without making psychology feel intimidating.

How can I contact you?

You can use the Contact page or email us directly at thepsyke111@gmail.com.

What’s it like to work with you?

Collaborative, curious, and youth-focused. We welcome ideas, writing submissions, and conversations that can make psychology more accessible.
