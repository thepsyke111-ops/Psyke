Learners, Today

Psyke! is a student-led psychology platform created to make learning psychology simpler, more engaging, and accessible to everyone.

The website offers chapter-wise notes and concept charts for Psychology NCERT, helping students understand key concepts and prepare for tests more effectively. Alongside academics, Psyke explores psychology-related topics, theories, concepts, and real-life perspectives through interesting and easy-to-read articles.

Psyke also provides a space for young writers and psychology enthusiasts to share their thoughts, ideas, and perspectives. Selected submissions are reviewed and approved before being published, ensuring that every article adds something meaningful to the Psyke community.

Learn psychology. Explore ideas. Find your perspective.

Meet the Founder

Preksha Bhatnagar is a psychology student with a deep curiosity about the human mind, human behaviour, and the many ways psychology shapes the world around us. Passionate about exploring the subject beyond textbooks, she believes that psychology can help us understand ourselves, connect with others, and approach the world with greater empathy and awareness. She strives to make the world a better place, little by little, through learning, sharing ideas, and creating spaces where young voices can be heard.

Beyond academics, Preksha has remained actively involved in initiatives that allow her to learn, lead, and contribute to her community. She has served as Head of Directors of her school's Interact Club, taking on responsibilities that strengthened her leadership, teamwork, and communication skills, and has also gained exposure through various internships. Through her experiences and interests, she hopes to combine her love for psychology with her passion for writing, social impact, and youth engagement, encouraging others to stay curious, think deeply, and believe that even small efforts can create meaningful change.
